A BETTER ENDING FOR EVERY ITEM

Know before
you throw.

Simple, practical disposal tips to help you sort waste with confidence.

♧
PLASTICS

Give plastic a clean start.

Empty containers, rinse residue, and check the recycling symbol before sorting.

  • Rinse bottles and tubs
  • Follow local cap rules
  • Keep plastic bags separate
⌁
E-WASTE

Keep electronics in the loop.

Electronics may contain valuable materials and components that need special handling.

  • Never put batteries in general bins
  • Use an authorized drop-off
  • Protect personal data first
▤
PAPER & CARDBOARD

Keep paper ready to recycle.

Dry, clean paper is easier to recover and reuse than wet or food-soiled material.

  • Flatten cardboard boxes
  • Keep paper dry
  • Remove food-contaminated pieces
◉
METAL & CANS

Recycle the valuable bits.

Metal is often recyclable, but local collection programs determine what they accept.

  • Empty and rinse cans
  • Handle sharp edges carefully
  • Check local accepted materials
♧ Local recycling rules vary. Use the collection center's guidance for final disposal decisions.
